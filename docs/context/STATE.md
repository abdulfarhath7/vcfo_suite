## Late-fee calculator, phase 1 (13 Nov 2026, on `PRITHISH`, not deployed)

- **What it does.** A filing that is late, or still unfiled after its due date, shows what its late fee has reached. Staff see it per client on the obligation record, with a total for the period in the head; the client sees it as "Late fee so far" on the filing detail. Hovering the amount shows the days late, the rule, the caveat and the law.
- **Rules.** `src/data/late-fee-rules.ts`. Kinds: `per-day` (with an optional cap), `fixed` and `maximum` (the most an authority may impose, shown as "up to").
