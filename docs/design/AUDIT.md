# Colour and type audit

Date: 21 October 2026. Scope: `src/` and `app/`, files `*.tsx`, `*.ts`, `*.css`. Counts are occurrences from `grep -rE`; "files" is the number of files that contain at least one hit. The client portal keeps its CSS twice (`css-src/` is the source, `css/` is generated by `scripts/cp-css.mjs`), so portal numbers are doubled in the raw totals; the per-file tables below use `css-src/` only.

## 2. Three token systems, one app

1. **`app/globals.css`**, lines 17 to 243 (light) and 292 onward (dark). OKLCH channel triplets, read through `tailwind.config.ts` as `oklch(var(--x) / <alpha-value>)`. Holds the shadcn names (`--background`, `--card`, `--muted`, `--accent`), a second "noir" set (`--ink`, `--panel`, `--raised`, `--paper`), a blue scale, a gold alias set, an orange alias set, an indigo scale, ten categorical `--accent-*` hues, eight `--stat-*` calendar hues, five `--phase-*` washes, `--role-accent-*` with per-role overrides, `--super-gold-*`, and five `--hex-loader-*` values in HSL.
2. **Tailwind's own palette.** Barely used (`blue-*` 105 hits, `emerald-*` 11, `indigo-*` 9, everything else 0 to 2), so it is not the problem; it just needs to stay unused.
3. **The client portal**, `src/components/client-portal/css-src/00-tokens.css` and `10-base.css`. Hand-ported from the prototype with its own names: `--bg --panel --raised --border --hair --fg --muted --subtle --primary --primary-dark --primary-light --navy --navy-ink --gold --gold-text --gold-soft --ok --due --over --prog --up --violet --sky --teal --you --with --own-you --own-sbc --folder --shadow --pop`, plus per-authority colours on credential cards (`[data-auth=mca|it|gst|oth]`). Its dark theme is `html[data-theme="dark"]` with `--bg: #000` ("true black"); the rest of the app's dark theme is `.dark, [data-theme="dark"]` on cool navy. Tailwind preflight is reverted inside the portal (`all: revert`) and `.cp-tw` islands keep it.

Nothing links system 1 and system 3. The same status ("overdue") is `--danger` in one and `--over` in the other, with different hues.

## 4. Structure rules broken

- **Gradients.** 15 in `globals.css` (hero, skeleton shimmer, page atmosphere), 10 in `shell-appearance.ts`, 7 in `Aurora.tsx`, 4 each in `LandingHero`, `LandingCta`, portal `Popups.tsx`, 3 each in `ProjectsSections`, `LandingImpact`, `Reader.tsx`, `CreateProjectPhasePath`, `app/roles/page.tsx`, `app/contact/page.tsx`; 29 across the portal CSS. Rule: no gradients on product surfaces. Marketing and auth pages are a later slice and may keep one restrained brand wash each.
- **Coloured left borders.** `AccentKpi.tsx` (five tones on a 3px left edge), `LoginShowcase.tsx`, `LoginFormCard.tsx`, `PreIncorporationNotice.tsx`, `views/client/Documents.tsx`. Rule: KPI cards carry a 3px top edge in their tone; notices use a hairline frame, a soft ground and an icon.
- **Cards in cards.** `DashSection` panels nest `.surface` blocks in several views (to be listed per slice during migration).
- **Shadows on rules and blur.** 93 `shadow-*` and 14 `backdrop-blur`. Rule: `shadow-card` on resting cards, `shadow-float` on floating layers, nothing else; no blur.
- **Radius scale is inverted.** `--radius` (lg) is 10px but `--radius-xl` is 8px and `--radius-2xl` 12px. Chips, avatars and dots are pills (`rounded-full` 213 hits). One scale: 6 / 8 / 10 / 12, pills only for toggle knobs and progress rings.
- **Weights.** 93 `font-extrabold` labels at 10.5 to 11.5px. The new scale stops at 600.

## 5. Type

- `app/layout.tsx` loads Manrope as `--font-sans`, Space Grotesk as `--font-serif` (the display hook; `.serif` class 470 hits, `font-serif` 34), IBM Plex Mono as `--font-mono`.
- The portal self-hosts Manrope 400 to 800 and Space Grotesk 500 to 700 from `public/client-portal/fonts/` and writes `font: ... Manrope` in about fifteen rules across `00-tokens.css`, `10-base.css`, `incorporation-extra.css`, `incorporation-landing.css`.
- The prototype intro uses Poppins; the logo spec names Plus Jakarta Sans. Four faces in one product.
- Decision already made: Public Sans for everything, IBM Plex Mono for identifiers. `--font-serif` stays as a variable for one release and points at the same Public Sans so the 470 `.serif` call sites keep working while they are migrated.

## 6. Dark mode

- App: `.dark, [data-theme="dark"]` on cool navy (`--background: 16% 0.042 258`).
- Portal: `html[data-theme="dark"]` on `#000`, with `[data-paper]` islands forced back to light.
- Rule: one dark theme, navy not black, both selectors kept during migration, `[data-paper]` kept for printed documents.

## 7. The prototype, for the record

`~/Downloads/Lead Dashboard.html` (v8) is the layout reference. Its lead screens use navy `#0f2a4a` for text (353 hits), `#1b4f94` for links (47), and the same accent mix this audit removes: red `#cf3a2f` 116, green `#15805f` 115, orange `#d9731f` 77, gold `#c9a24b` 35, cream `#fbf3df` 40, plus 424 inline `oklch(` values. Font sizes on the Today screen run 9.5 to 14px with a 21px title; radii are 6px on cards and chips, 99px on pills. The density is right; the colours are not.

## 8. What the merge looks like

Every token in the three systems maps to one of 37 new colour tokens (see `TOKENS.md`, section "Deprecated names"). Old names stay as aliases of the new tokens for one release, so the 2,165 `oklch(var(--old))` references keep resolving while call sites move. The portal's `00-tokens.css` keeps its selector structure but its values become `var(--new-token)`; `cp-css.mjs` does not change.
