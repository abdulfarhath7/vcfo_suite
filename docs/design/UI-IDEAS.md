# UI ideas for the prototype

Collected on 2026-10-22 for the UI/UX-only prototype. Each row is one way to present something the app already has (the compliance calendar, the 34-step incorporation journey, charts, timelines, dashboards, the client portal) so that it looks good and reads fast. The "Moodboard reference" column points at files in `~/Documents/reference/vcfo-suite/finance-ui-refs/` (`dribbble NN` is the shot number in `dribbble/`, a bare name such as `vanta` is `sites/<name>-hero.png`, and `solcis capture` is `solcis/`). Effort is a rough size for a prototype screen: S is under a day, M is one to three days, L is a week or more.

The intern's month-wheel idea is row 1. Rows are grouped by area; pick one or two per area for the prototype rather than building all of them.

## How to use this list

1. Pick the three areas that matter most for the demo (the compliance calendar, the incorporation journey and the home dashboard) and choose two ideas from each.
2. Sketch each pick as one screen in the prototype and compare it against the moodboard image named in its row.
3. Keep the visual language rows (the mono figures, the one-accent palette, the dot chips and the hairline cards) as fixed rules; they are what makes the other ideas look like one product.
4. Use the motion rows sparingly: scroll reveal, count-up numbers and ring fills are enough; the pointer-reactive canvas belongs only on the login and marketing pages.
