Question 1

Who does what in one SPICe+ filing

Green steps are legal for the agent to do. Red steps stay with a person, by law. The line between them is what keeps the tool legal.

✓ Agent may doConditions apply✋ Person only

The order, step by step

  1. Person logs in with their own ID, captcha and OTP.
  2. Agent pulls the client profile and the signed recipe.
  3. Agent fills the fields, then saves, checks and downloads the PDF.
  4. Person checks every field against the originals.
  5. Each signer signs with their own DSC.
  6. Agent attaches the exact signed file.
  7. Person clicks Submit and pays.
  8. Agent reads the SRN and saves it with the run log.

Conditions on every green step

  • A person is present and starts each fill.
  • No retry loops; back off on errors.
  • Never a dummy value in a mandatory field.
  • No bulk or unattended runs.
  • Only the firm's own clients, only verified records.
  • Written CS sign-off before any live use.
Sources: MCA Website Policies (2018 copy); IT Act via India Code; CCA DSC custody rules2

Why the GST portal is different

GSTN's terms allow access "only through the interfaces expressly authorised by GSTN" and call scraping an offence. MCA's terms have no such clause; their only automation rule targets "unusually high numbers of daily database accesses". So each new portal needs its own review.

Sources: MCA Website Policies; ICAI DC/1891/2024; GSTN notice via TaxHeal; MCA V3 and ICSI FAQs3

Why it is a problem today, not in 2027

CA Act, Second Schedule, Part I, Clause (1): disclosing client information "to any person other than his client ... without the consent of his client" is professional misconduct. In force now.

SPDI Rules (IT Act s.43A): transfers of sensitive data need consent or necessity. In force until ~May 2027.

Anthropic Usage Policy (from 12 Nov 2026): personal legal or tax advice is high-risk. A qualified person reviews the answer first, and the person is told AI was used.

Evidence limits

  • mca.gov.in and the Gazette blocked access.
  • MCA terms read from an official 2018 copy.
  • SPDI Rules, Aadhaar s.29, AWS DPA and ICAI's AI note not read in full.
  • Anthropic's two retention statements conflict.
  • Whether India-only processing works on the endpoint your code uses must be checked in the AWS Mumbai console.
  • No court has ruled on attended browser filling.

The bottom line

The extension, which looked like the legal risk, is the most careful tool found on the market. The AI chat inside your own app is the part already exposed today, because of the oldest rule in the profession: do not share a client's affairs without the client's consent. So the next six months are a sequencing problem, not a legal block. First: client consent and the CERT-In runbook. Then: Bedrock India and director notices before May 2027. And before any live filing: a gate in Suite that will not show the Submit card until the certifier's own evidence is attached.

Research, not legal advice. A practising Company Secretary or advocate must read the full report, check its sources and confirm each verdict in writing before live use of VCFO Assist or real-data use of Ask VCFO.
