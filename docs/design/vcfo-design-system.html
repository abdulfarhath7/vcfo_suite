Brand book · v1.5, the Portal refresh

VCFO Suite

Incorporation and compliance for Indian companies, run by SBC for its clients. A calm grey canvas, white cards on thin neutral hairlines, a navy for the main action, and colour only where it tells you something.

Every swatch, control and screen on this page is drawn from the tokens at the top of this file, which carry the Portal refresh values. Switch the theme above; every part follows. docs/design/design-tokens.json still holds the v1.4 values until the token merge, and the deprecated-names table at the end lists what moves.

Content

Six people use the product: the lead (an SBC associate who runs a client's incorporation), the manager, the firm admin, the super admin, the developer who runs SBC's setup, and the client (a founder, often on a phone). The sample data throughout is Northwind Analytics India Pvt Ltd and its lead, Arjun Mehta.

  • Plain English. Say what happened: "Waiting for Northwind", "Due 24 Oct", "11 of 14 steps done". No jargon, no "Submit", no exclamation marks, no emoji.
  • Sentence case for titles, buttons, chips, labels, table heads and group titles. Nothing is set in capitals by a style; acronyms (COI, GSTR-1, TDS) and names keep their own case.
  • Buttons are verbs with their object: "Approve request", "Email manager", "Open incorporation". Two words where possible, never a wrapped label.
  • Dates are real dates, D Mon ("24 Oct", "Wed 21 Oct 2026"); times as "10:42 am". A late date is the plain date in red, not "3 d overdue". Never "in 5 days" for a statutory deadline.
  • No money on dashboards. Amounts appear only on the pages that are about them, with the rupee sign and Indian grouping (₹9,05,178) in tabular figures, right-aligned in tables.
  • Identifiers (CIN, PAN, DIN, GSTIN, SRN, file names) are the only text that may be set in the mono face.
  • Status is a word. A colour never carries meaning alone; a chip always has its word beside its dot.

Foundations

Colour

The page is a cool grey canvas (surface, #F3F5F9). Cards are white (surface-raised) inside a neutral line (#E3E6EB); rows inside a card are split by the lighter line-soft (#EEF0F3). Text is ink (#16212F) for titles and figures, ink-2 for row text, ink-muted for meta and labels, and ink-faint only for counts, ticks and placeholders.

Navy carries the weight. The sidebar and the greeting band are navy (#15263B and #1B3049). The main button and the picked tab are navy-action (#1F3B5C), and table heads sit on navy-tint (#EEF2F8). The blue brand (#2563A8, soft #E6EFFA) is for links, the current step, the current Gantt bar and blue chips; it is not the main button.

Status colours each come with a soft tint: green done (#1E7F55 on #E2F3EA), amber waiting or due (#A35F00 on #FCEFD8), red overdue (#BE3434 on #FBE6E6), grey planned or idle (#56627A on #ECEFF4). Violet (#6A4FB6) and teal (#0F7C86) are category colours for icon tiles, the MCA owner chip and chart series. The brand orange (#E9792F) is for small highlights only: the today line, the COI diamond and the active nav rail.

Colour is information, never decoration. It sits in small shapes: a chip, a dot, an icon tile, a date tile, a bar. There are no gradient or gold bars on cards, strips or heads, and no coloured accent borders; every frame and divider is a thin neutral hairline.

Type

Public Sans for everything, weights 400, 500 and 600, with 650 only for the day in a date tile and a large countdown. Figures are tabular. The scale runs 10.5, 12, 12.5, 13.5, 14.5, 16, 20, 22, 28px. IBM Plex Mono is allowed for identifiers only.

Shape

Radii 5 (chips), 6 (date tiles, small buttons, the picked tab), 8 (buttons, icon tiles, the tab track), 10 (cards), 12 (sheets and screens). Dots and status circles are round; everything else is a rounded rectangle. Cards have a hairline and no shadow; shadow-float is for things that really float: menus, popovers, toasts.

Weight

The navy sidebar, the navy greeting band and the navy main button are the only heavy elements. They give the screen its weight so nothing else has to.

Client colours

Each client company owns one colour, the same on every screen: the dot beside its name, its initials tile, its rows in Work, Vault and the calendar. A project lead running eight companies recognises a company by its colour before reading the name. People, tags and nav items stay neutral, and a client colour never carries a state. This is the owner's decision and holds in the refresh.

Rules

"I don't like the border line colours, they don't look professional."The owner, 26 October 2026, on the coloured top edges and accent lines of v1.4.
  • Colour only as information. Two kinds of colour carry meaning: a state, and a client company. Category colours (violet, teal) only tint icon tiles, owner chips and chart series.
  • No decorative colour lines. No gradient or gold bar on a card, a strip or a head. No coloured top edge on a number, no coloured left border on a card or a notice. Frames and dividers are thin neutral hairlines (line, line-soft).
  • The two coloured edges that remain are information: the 3px red edge of the one urgent row in an attention list, and the orange rail on the active sidebar item.
  • Status is a soft chip with a dot and a word. No solid status blocks anywhere, in cards or in tables.
  • The main button is navy. One main button per region; everything else is secondary or ghost.
  • Buttons in one row share one height; chips in one column share one width (status 108px, owner 96px). Labels never wrap.
  • Every card has the same head: 52px, a hairline under it, a 28px tinted icon tile chosen from the title, the title at 14.5px, controls on the right. Rows inside a card run the full width with hairlines between them. No cards inside cards.
  • Tabs never wrap. When a head is too narrow, the counts drop out first, then the title shortens with an ellipsis.
  • Hover goes one step darker or to the surface-sunken tint. Disabled is 45% opacity, never a different colour. Motion is 150ms ease on hover and toggles only.
  • Every interactive element gets the 2px focus-ring outline, offset 2px.
  • Pages have no title. The breadcrumb and the active nav item already name the page; the first row of the page is its controls, starting at the left edge.
  • Every breadcrumb segment except the current page is a link. Back goes to the previous screen, never out of the product.
  • Sidebar items are sorted by priority: what the person opens most comes first.
  • Dark mode is slate, not black. Chips and tiles mix their colour with the panel instead of switching to a new hue.

Colour tokens

Light and dark are two values of one name. Each card shows the token name used in code, the name the Portal refresh document gives it, and both values. Muted text holds 4.9:1 on white and 4.5:1 on the canvas. On their own tints, the blue, red, violet and grey chip texts hold 4.7 to 5.3:1; the green, amber and teal chip texts sit at 4.3 to 4.4:1, which is under the 4.5:1 line for small text, so those three need a check before the token merge. Faint text is 2.6:1 and is never body text.

Eight hues, one per client, assigned when the engagement is created and never changed. Each has a dot and text colour and a soft tile ground. They mark identity only: a status is always a status chip with a word, never a client colour.

Every page below the greeting-card dashboards opens on its controls, not on a title. The top bar carries a back button, the logo, the breadcrumb, the preview switch, search with its "Ctrl K" shortcut, the bells and the theme toggle.

Buttons

The main button is navy-action (#1F3B5C, hover #18304C) with white text, 36px tall, 8px corners, 13.5px at 600. It is never blue. Small buttons are 30px for rows and dense toolbars; 40px is for every button on a phone. Every text button is at least 84px wide except small row actions. One main button per region.

Tabs

One segmented control for every switch: views, filters inside a card, the theme toggle at the top of this page. Small inline buttons sit on a quiet track (#F5F6F8, a 1px line, 2px padding, 8px corners). Each button is 28px tall, 12.5px at 600, in ink-muted. The picked one is navy-action with white text. A count follows the label in faint grey (#98A1AE), and in #AFC3DA on the picked one. There is no sliding highlight; the picked button changes colour in place.

Tabs never wrap. In a card head, the tabs sit on the right and their buttons tighten to 8px side padding; when the head is still too narrow the counts drop out (right), then the title shortens with an ellipsis.

Chips

One chip shape for every mark: a soft tint behind text of the same hue, a 6px round dot, 22px tall, 8px side padding, 5px corners, 12px at 600. There are no solid status chips. Status chips in a table column are 108px wide; owner chips are 96px wide. Labels are centred and never wrap.

Inputs

36px, the same height as a button. Label above, helper below, line-strong border, brand border and the focus ring on focus. Error text goes under the field in danger with the problem itself.

Filters

Show only the filters a person needs every time, usually one or two, as compact dropdowns beside the view switch. The label names the current choice ("All clients", "Brightwave", "3 clients"); a filter off its default takes the navy-tint look. A multi-select list has a Clear link and keeps client dots; a single choice closes on pick. Everything else (templates, export) goes into a More menu. Never a row of chips for every value, never a date chip that repeats the page.

Section card

Every panel on every screen: white, a 1px line frame, 10px corners, no shadow. The head is at least 52px tall with a line-soft hairline under it. It holds a 28px icon tile (8px corners, a 15px glyph in the tone, the tint behind it), the title at 14.5px/600 in ink, an optional meta in muted 12.5px, and the controls on the right (tabs, a link, a small button). The body has 16px by 20px padding; lists inside it run the full card width with hairlines between rows. When two cards always sit together and talk about the same thing, they become one card with tabs.

The icon tile comes from the title

The tile is not chosen per screen. The title is read against the list below, top to bottom, and the first match sets the glyph; each glyph has one tone, so a card means the same thing on every screen. A title that matches nothing gets the blue grid. A head that already draws its own tile keeps it, recoloured by the same rule.

Numbers are split by line-soft hairlines. There is no coloured top edge and no gradient line. The strip sits under the greeting band, or on its own where a page has no greeting.

Greeting card

One component on every dashboard, staff and client. A navy band (a gradient from #1B3049 to #15263B) holds the date in #9FB2C8 and the name at 20px/600 in white; the client's band holds the company name. Under it, a white strip of numbers, each with its 30px tinted icon tile, the label (12px muted) above the number (20px ink, red when overdue), split by hairlines. There is no colour line, no ring and no marker on the card. The numbers roll in once when the page opens. Where a month-on-month change matters (super admin), it follows the number in green or red. On a phone the band and the strip stack the same way, two numbers to a row.

Changes in v1.5

From the "VCFO Portal Refresh" document and the owner's review on 26 October 2026 ("follow the rules for each and every card"). The clickable prototype follows these rules through docs/specs/landing-page/src/proto-fix/patch.mjs and ds.css; what is still open is listed in docs/specs/landing-page/REFRESH-DIFF.md.

  • Refresh palette. Canvas #F3F5F9, white cards, hairlines #E3E6EB and #EEF0F3, ink #16212F, accent blue #2563A8, statuses green, amber and red with soft tints, violet and teal as category colours, navy #15263B for the sidebar and the greeting. The brand orange #E9792F is for small marks only. Gold is gone.
  • No decorative colour lines. The gradient bars on cards and strips, the gold underline, the 3px top edge on stat cards and the 2px ink rule under table heads are removed. Colour is information only.
  • Navy main button. The main button is navy-action #1F3B5C, 36px (small 30px), not blue.
  • Soft chips everywhere. No solid status blocks. A chip is a soft tint with a 6px dot, 22px, 5px corners; status chips in tables are 108px, owner chips 96px (client amber, SBC blue, MCA violet).
  • Card heads with icon tiles. This reverses v1.3. Every head is 52px with a hairline, a 28px tinted icon tile chosen from the title, a 14.5px title and the controls on the right.
  • New tabs. Small inline buttons on a quiet track, the picked one navy, counts in faint grey, no sliding highlight, never wrapping.
  • Greeting card. The navy band stays; the numbers in the white strip each get a 30px icon tile, label above the number, red when overdue. No ring and no marker.
  • Attention list for Important alerts and What is next: 44px date tiles tinted by urgency, an urgent row with a red wash, a red edge and a small navy button.
  • Timeline in the refresh Gantt shape: status circles, 16px rounded bars, a hatched late part, an orange today line and an orange COI diamond.
  • Charts. One stacked bar with a legend instead of a donut; phase progress as a 6px bar per phase with the count under it.
  • Incorporation counts 25 steps in 3 phases; registrations have their own page. The COI card is navy with one bar split by phase and no ring.
  • Dark mode is slate (#111821, #18202B, #1F2935), not black; chips and tiles mix their colour with the panel.
  • Developer console in the lcc Settings shape: a section rail, cards of label and value rows, settings that save as they change.
  • Smaller things. Search shows "Ctrl K"; sidebar items are sorted by priority; the Ask VCFO character moved inside the chat panel; sentence case everywhere, including group titles and table heads.

Changes in v1.4

From the owner's review on 26 October 2026. Rules that v1.5 replaces are marked.

  • Navy greeting is back on every portal, with the numbers in a white strip under the band.
  • Tables: pale navy head in sentence case; state as a soft pill with a dot. (v1.5 drops the 2px ink rule under the head.)
  • Tasks: checkbox and one status icon; a late task shows a red date, not the word "Overdue".
  • Three timelines, one per job: the Gantt for a plan over dates; the vertical stepper for the steps of an incorporation step; the two-sided thread (authority left, SBC right, date tiles on a spine) for a registration or an obligation.
  • Detail pages open with a deadline band: a ring, two or three dates, who is on it, and one action that shows working, then done.
  • Words: "Remind" and "Reminder", never "Nudge". No money on any dashboard.
  • Motion, three moments only: numbers roll in, a new Inbox item slides in, the live status pill at the top while work runs. Reduced motion turns all three off.
  • Settings live in Profile, opened from the account row; firm-wide settings stay with the admin as Firm settings.

Order of sections

The screen answers questions in reading order: what needs me, what is late, what is coming, who is waiting, then the long lists. The headline numbers go in the greeting strip and never again as a band of tiles under it. The side rail holds the status split (a stacked bar) and the waiting list.

Rows and lists

A row inside a section runs the full card width: flex items-center gap-3 min-h-row px-5 border-t border-line-soft first:border-0, a neutral 26px tile with initials, the name at 13.5px/500, meta at 12px ink-muted under it, a chip at the column width, an optional 30px secondary button. More than eight rows: a DashDataTable or a link to the full page. A list with nothing in it says what will appear there.

Before calling it done

  • Only tokens on this page (client colours come from the client set); rg "oklch\([0-9]|accent-(amber|rose|violet|emerald|sky|teal|pink|cyan|lime)|border-l-|border-t-\[3px\]|bg-gradient-to-r|rounded-full px-|font-extrabold" over the new files returns nothing.
  • npm run typecheck green, npx eslint --quiet clean, npm run test passing.
  • Screenshots in light and dark at 1440px and 375px look like the screens above, with slate (not black) in dark.
  • One navy main button per region; every status has a word; no colour line on any card, strip or head.
  • No page title; every breadcrumb segment links; Back returns to the previous screen.

Never

Decorative colour linesNo gradient or gold bar on a card, a strip or a head; no coloured top edge on a number; no coloured underline. Frames and dividers are neutral hairlines.
Coloured accent bordersNo coloured left or top border on a card, a notice or a tile. The one urgent row in an attention list is the only red edge.
Solid status blocksA state is a soft chip with a dot and a word, in cards and in tables alike. No white text on a status fill.
Blue main buttonsThe main button is navy-action. Blue is for links and the current step.
Orange as text or actionThe brand orange marks the today line, the COI diamond and the active nav rail. It is never text, a button or a fill behind words.
Sliding tab highlightsThe picked tab changes colour in place. Tabs never wrap onto a second line.
Donuts for a splitA split is one stacked bar with a legend.
Black dark modeDark mode is slate (#111821). Chips and tiles mix with the panel; they do not glow.
Colour on people and tagsPeople, tags and nav items are neutral. Only states and client companies are coloured, and a client colour never stands in for a state.
Page titlesA heading that repeats the nav item and the breadcrumb. The page opens on its controls.
Dead breadcrumbsA breadcrumb segment that is not a link, or a Back that leaves the product.
Cards inside cardsRows inside a panel are hairline-separated. One raised surface per panel.
Zig-zag columnsChips and buttons in a column share one width and centred labels. Labels never wrap.
Capitals and weight 700No uppercase labels or heads. Public Sans stops at 600, with 650 only for date-tile days and a countdown.
