Incorporation Documents Map

The details the client and the project lead enter during incorporation, the step each one comes from, and the documents the app fills in from them. Nobody retypes a detail: it is entered once and printed wherever it is needed.

The steps, in order

  1. Before Project created Company name, type, parent, client login SBC team
  2. Phase 1 · Step 1 Client Details Parent, signatory, names, capital, directors Client
  3. Phase 1 · Step 2 Draft Board Resolution The app drafts it; the lead reviews Project lead
  4. Phase 1 · Step 3 Signed Board Resolution Signed on the parent’s letterhead Client
  5. Phase 1 · Step 4 Name Application Filed with the ROC Project lead
  6. Phase 1 · Step 5 Name Approval Approved name and MCA letter Project lead
  7. Phase 2 · Step 1 Capital Structure Share classes, counts, values Client
  8. Phase 2 · Step 2 Registered Office Address, owner NOC, utility bill Client
  9. Phase 2 · Step 3 Proposed Directors One entry per director, with KYC Client
  10. Phase 2 · Step 4 Subscriber Details Who takes the shares Client
  11. Phase 2 · Step 5 KYC Review & DSC Documents are generated and shared here; signed copies come back Project lead
  12. Phase 2 · Step 6 SPICe+ Confirmation Client confirms the filing Client
  13. Phase 2 · Step 7 SPICe+ Filing Filed on the MCA portal Project lead
  14. Phase 2 · Step 8 MCA Remarks Resubmission, if asked Project lead
  15. Phase 2 · Step 9 Certificate of Incorporation CIN, PAN, TAN shared with the client Project lead
