Your complete compliance companion.

One web app. SBC uses it to set up a client's company and to keep that company compliant.

The client journey

1
Start
  • Staff create the project
  • Client gets a login
  • Client answers a questionnaire
2
Prepare
  • 34-step SPICe+ checklist
  • Directors, capital, office
  • KYC and DSC
3
Documents
  • App makes MCA drafts
  • MOA, AOA, DIR-2, letters
  • Board resolution
4
Incorporate
  • Filing with MCA
  • COI received
  • Company is live
5
Stay compliant
  • Yearly compliance calendar
  • Due-date reminders
  • Filings register
