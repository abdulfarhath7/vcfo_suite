What happens to a company, start to finish.

Four stages happen once. The calendar repeats every year. The firm runs every step; the client sees every step, with who holds it and when it is due.

  1. Name and papers5 steps, about 2 weeks. Client and firm.
  2. Incorporation11 steps, SPICe+ with MCA. Firm and MCA.
  3. Certificate of IncorporationDay 0. CIN, PAN and TAN arrive.
  4. The first 180 days11 steps with statutory clocks. The firm.
  5. Registrations23 items, only the ones that apply. The firm.
  6. Compliance, every yearMonthly, quarterly and annual filings. Firm and client.

{{ICON:circle-dot}}Every node on this line is a page in the portal, and every step on that page shows the same three things: who holds it, when it is due, and the document that proves it.

∞Every year

Then the calendar takes over.

A private company files something almost every week of the year. Every filing on this ring has a due date, an owner and a nudge in the portal. Nothing depends on someone remembering.

What a financial year looks like

  • GST. GSTR-1 by the 11th and GSTR-3B by the 20th, every month. GSTR-9 once a year.
  • Income tax. TDS paid by the 7th each month, TDS returns each quarter, advance tax in June, September, December and March, Form 16 in June.
  • MCA. MBP-1 and DIR-8 in April, MSME-1 twice a year, DPT-3 in June, DIR-3 KYC in September, AOC-4 and MGT-7 after the annual general meeting.
  • Labour. Professional tax by the 10th, PF and ESI by the 15th, every month.
  • RBI. The FLA return by 15 July for every company with foreign capital.

Questions clients ask before the first step.

Who is VCFO Suite for?{{ICON:chevron-down}}

Firms that incorporate and keep companies compliant in India, and their clients: Indian founders, and foreign parents setting up a subsidiary or a global capability centre. The firm, the Indian team and the parent entity all log in to the same record.

Is it only for SBC?{{ICON:chevron-down}}

No. SBC built it and runs its engagements on it, which is why every screen on this page is a real one. Another firm gets its own workspace, with its own people, clients and calendar.

What does the client actually do in the portal?{{ICON:chevron-down}}

Answer the questionnaire, upload the documents each step asks for, approve board resolutions, sign with a DSC, and watch the timeline. Questions go to Ask VCFO or to the people named on the engagement.

Which filings does the calendar cover?{{ICON:chevron-down}}

MCA, GST, Income tax, Payroll, FEMA and Labour obligations for the financial year, generated from the certificate date and the registrations the company holds. Each one carries its statutory due date.

How are we reminded?{{ICON:chevron-down}}

Every due date emails the owner of the step. With consent, a WhatsApp reminder goes out as well. The email is always the record; WhatsApp never replaces it.

Where is our data hosted, and who can see it?{{ICON:chevron-down}}

On AWS in the Mumbai region, with encrypted storage. Access is scoped by role, so a client sees only their own companies and the firm's people assigned to them.
