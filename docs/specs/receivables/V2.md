# Receivables v2 — Tally import, approval, People tab, emails, WhatsApp

Built 17 Nov 2026 on `feature/receivables-v2` from the owner's brief (`RECEIVABLES-TALLY-BUILD-BRIEF.md`).
Decisions taken during the build are in `V2-DECISIONS.md`; what changed, file by file, is in
`V2-BUILD-REPORT.md`. `PLAN.md` still describes v1; where the two differ, this file wins.

## What it is now

- Receivables is an internal, staff-only tool. Client partners and leadership never sign in. They
  are typed-in names and email addresses.
- Summit's accountant exports the Tally **Sales Register** as Excel. A staff member uploads it.
- The rows wait in **Awaiting approval** at the top of the Sheet tab, grouped by partner, then client.
- Approved rows become invoices, or change the amount of an existing invoice.
- Amount changes that a partner sends by email are entered with **Change amount**. They go through
  the same approval.
- Emails go automatically to client partners and leadership. Statements go to clients only when
  someone clicks.
- Every email goes from someone's Outlook. A WhatsApp message (DoubleTick) then tells that person
  what went out.
- Payments are marked received by hand. The HSBC feed and the due-date reminders are gone.
- Partner logins, the portal, share links and publish alerts are hidden behind
  `RECEIVABLES_EXTERNAL`, which is off by default.

## Architecture

```
  Tally ── Sales Register.xlsx ──▶ POST /api/receivables/sheets/:id/imports
                                        │ parseTallySalesRegister (pure)
                                        ▼
                                 imports.ts stageTallyImport
                                        │ receivable_import_batches + receivable_import_rows
                                        ▼                       (one pending batch per sheet)
  Change amount ─▶ POST /invoices/:id/amount-change ─▶ receivable_import_rows (source=staff)
                                        │
                     Approval panel ─▶ POST …/imports/approve ─▶ approveImportRows (1 transaction)
                                        │                         │ receivable_invoices (new / amount, revision)
                                        │                         │ receivable_clients (credit days, partner)
                                        │                         │ receivable_changes (logChange)
                                        ▼
                    queueApprovalEmails ─▶ Inngest "receivables/invoices.approved"
                                        │   (falls back to after() without an event key)
                                        ▼
         receivables-approval-emails ─▶ sendApprovalEmails ─▶ email A / A′ from the approver's Outlook
  cron :05 receivables-rollup ──────────▶ sendSheetRollups  ─▶ email B from the manager's Outlook
  cron :10 receivables-leadership-digest▶ sendSheetDigest   ─▶ email C from the manager's Outlook
  People ▸ Send statement ─▶ POST /clients/:id/statement ─▶ after() ─▶ email D from the clicker's Outlook
                                        │
              every email: receivable_email_log row (sending ▸ sent / failed), then
                                        ▼
         Inngest "receivables/email.sent" ─▶ receivables-email-ping ─▶ pingSender
                                        │                               │ DoubleTick API
                                        ▼                               ▼
                       email_log.whatsapp_status          the sender's own WhatsApp
```

The repository seam is unchanged. Every new function that takes an `AuthContext` goes through
`scopedSheet`. The `system*` functions are called only by the jobs, and by `after()` work that a
scoped route has already checked.

- An automatic email goes only when two switches are on: its sheet switch on the People tab, and
  the partner's own switch.
- Every switch starts off.
- Without a connected Outlook, the email is logged as `failed` with `outlook_not_connected`.
- The colours come from the Spectrum email tokens. The token `brand` is the app's purple, so the
  SBC orange is `highlight` (the header fill) and `highlight-bright` (the edge and the total rule).

## The Tally file

- The file is `.xlsx` inside, even when it is named `.xls`.
- A real BIFF `.xls` is refused with `not_xlsx` ("Export from Tally as Excel (.xlsx)").
- The column row is found by the cell "Voucher No.". It is not assumed to be row 12.
- Rows whose voucher type is not "Sales" are skipped, and so is the "Grand Total" row.
- The ledger columns are ignored.
- Mapping:
  - client = Buyer, falling back to Particulars;
  - invoice no. = Voucher No., exactly as written;
  - amount = Gross Total in INR, foreign clients included;
  - GSTIN = GSTIN/UIN.
- Voucher numbers match by a normalised key. Leading zeros are dropped in each number, so
  `SCSPL/26-27/0164` matches `SCSPL/26-27/164`.
- Clients match by `client_key`: lower case, with punctuation and spaces collapsed.

## WhatsApp (DoubleTick)

- The client is ported from Litigation Command Center (`lcc/src-tauri/src/notify/whatsapp.rs`,
  decision D-120 there): base URL `https://public.doubletick.io`, a raw-key `Authorization`
  header, and positional template placeholders.
- `src/lib/notify/send-whatsapp-doubletick.ts` reads `DOUBLETICK_*` on the server only.
- `WHATSAPP_PROVIDER=doubletick` sends the app's other WhatsApp notifications through it too. The
  default, `aws`, changes nothing.
- The receivables ping always uses DoubleTick when it is configured. It goes to the sender's
  `profiles.phone_e164`. Its outcome is recorded on the email log row: `sent`, `failed`,
  `no_phone` or `disabled`.
- The ping needs the approved template `DOUBLETICK_TEMPLATE_RECEIVABLES_EMAIL_SENT`. Its body has
  four placeholders: subject, recipient name, recipient email, and what the email was about.
- Without that template, the ping is plain text. WhatsApp delivers plain text only within 24 hours
  of the person's last message to the firm's number.

  - New tables:
    - `receivable_clients`;
    - `receivable_digest_recipients`;
    - `receivable_import_batches`;

  plain column.
- `receivable_reminders` stays in place, unused. It can be dropped later.
